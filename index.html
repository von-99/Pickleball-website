Pickleball Basics

What is Pickleball?

Pickleball is a sport played with paddles, a plastic ball, a net, and a court.

Pickleball is popular because it is fun and easy to learn. It can be played with two or four players.

Pickleball Fact

Pickleball was invented in 1965 in Washington state. It started as a game for families to play together.

Equipment Needed

  • Pickleball paddle
  • Pickleball
  • Net
  • Court
  • Athletic shoes
